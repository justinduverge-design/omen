# Spec: nflverse-data Research

## Status

Research queued

## Question

Can nflverse-data help Corvus reduce or delay the need for SportsRadar?

## Current Answer

nflverse may help with historical/statistical NFL data, but it is not a full SportsRadar replacement.

## Potential Uses

- historical player stats
- weekly stats
- schedules
- rosters
- player IDs
- fantasy-relevant baselines
- long-term analytics

## Likely Gaps

- real-time live data
- official commercial SLA
- reliable current injury/news feeds
- fantasy platform user rosters
- betting odds
- paid-grade projections

## Research Questions

1. Which nflverse datasets help Omen of the Week?
2. Which datasets help Trade Analyzer?
3. Which datasets help Draft Assistant?
4. Which datasets help Start/Sit?
5. How fresh is the data?
6. What formats are available?
7. Is licensing/attribution compatible with Corvus?
8. What does it not solve compared with SportsRadar?

## Decision Rule

Use nflverse only if it reduces cost or improves analytics without adding too much complexity.

Do not integrate before a research report exists.
