How Omen makes a call
Every week Omen reads your league and the whole NFL, then picks the one move worth making for each of your teams. If nothing is worth it, it says hold.
Omen's read
Omen forecasts every player from usage, matchup, game script and role changes. It also keeps score: for each position and scoring, whichever forecast, Omen's or your provider's, has been closer this season leads.
Confidence and risk
Strong means the reads agree. Lean means one pulls the other way. Risk is how bad the downside is.
The Ledger
Every call is graded on your league's own scoring, against the player it passed over.
