# Content & Marketing Done (cross-cutting — L1 work)

Apply to any public-facing post / video / social / marketing artifact.

## Gates

1. Supports the product (links to feature, result, or reason to return)
2. Doesn't overpromise — no fake guarantees, no gambling-style language
3. Matches Corvus voice (sharp, observant, plain English, recommendation first)
4. Avoids political satire, cussing, inappropriate scenes, real-person attacks
5. Clean enough to represent the brand (per content boundaries in `slops-saloon/Direction/content-strategy.md` when it lands)
6. Brand line used (if any) is approved per `Brand/brand-system.md` §2 — banned lines flagged
7. Saveable, reusable, measurable — filed to `slops-saloon/Solutions/marketing/published/` or `slops-saloon/Solutions/content/published/`
8. Performance tracking row created in `slops-saloon/Solutions/marketing/performance-reports/` or equivalent

## AAA mapping

- **Accuracy:** 1, 2, 6
- **Aesthetic:** 3, 4, 5, 7, 8
