# Corvus Brand

## Identity

Corvus is the Fantasy Football MVP product from Slops Saloon.

The brand should feel sharp, observant, trustworthy, and fantasy-football specific.

## Product Role

Corvus is a decision layer, not a general chatbot.

It helps users decide what to do next:

- evaluate a trade
- prepare for a draft
- choose the best weekly move
- understand start/sit and waiver implications inside the weekly decision flow

## Voice

Use plain English. Avoid burying the user in math.

Explain the recommendation, the evidence, the risk, and the confidence.

## Brand Boundaries

- Slops Saloon is the umbrella.
- `slops-saloon` is the Fantasy Sports MVP Builder department.
- Corvus is the fantasy football product.
