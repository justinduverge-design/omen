# ADR-009: September 10, 2026 Launch Deadline

**Date:** 2026-05-09
**Status:** Accepted
**Deciders:** Justin Duverge (CEO/Founder)

## Context

Fantasy football is an acutely seasonal product. The NFL regular season begins September 10, 2026. Missing this date means waiting a full year before paid launch is viable. The deadline is not negotiable — it is set by the calendar, not by the product team.

## Decision

September 10, 2026 is the hard production launch deadline for Corvus with MVP Move, all three platforms (Yahoo, Sleeper, ESPN), Stripe live keys, and VORP-based recommendations live. Every roadmap item is evaluated against this date. If a feature cannot ship by September 10 at AAA standard, it is deferred.

## Consequences

**Positive:**
- Forces disciplined prioritization — nothing ships below standard just to make the date
- Creates a shared clock that prevents scope creep and roadmap drift
- Aligns Corvus with the moment users are most engaged with fantasy football

**Negative / Trade-offs:**
- Any major unexpected technical blocker (Yahoo API change, Supabase outage, etc.) compresses the timeline with no buffer
- Forced cuts may affect product completeness (waiver wire, Fantrax are deferrable)

**Constraints:**
- All three platforms must be production-ready by launch — no partial paid product
- Roadmap changes that affect the September 10 deadline require Justin approval
- Security hardening, VORP, Start/Sit, and Stripe validation are never cut
