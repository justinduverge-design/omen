# Omen ESPN Scoring Disclosure v1

**Status:** Founder-directed — 2026-08-24

## Rule

Every ESPN recommendation must visibly carry **Exact ESPN scoring unavailable** until Omen has a lawful, validated path to capture every material league rule and reconcile the calculated result against the final ESPN result.

## Approved wording

> **Exact ESPN scoring unavailable**
>
> Omen may recognize some league settings, but it cannot yet verify every scoring rule and final ESPN result for this league. Any point-based guidance is not an exact final-score calculation.

## Meaning

- A reception setting alone is not a scoring contract.
- A complete football scoring contract covers every material offensive, kicking, team-defense, individual-defense, return, turnover, bonus, threshold, roster, and provider-adjustment rule.
- This is neutral capability transparency. Do not blame the user, ESPN, or a commissioner, and do not imply an estimate is verified exact scoring.

## Boundary

This disclosure does not authorize expanded ESPN access, scraping, a collector, timer, credential, SQL, migration, deployment, or production scoring action.
