# Omen Football Data Policy v1.3

**Audience:** Founder and product leadership
**Status:** Adopted by founder — 2026-08-24
**Purpose:** amend the adopted football-data policy with Omen’s recommendation-independence rule.

This document is an amendment to policies v1, v1.1, and v1.2. Their rules remain in force.

## The commitment

Omen’s recommendations, rankings, explanations, confidence, and product language are not for sale.

No advertiser, affiliate, provider, league, player, partner, commercial arrangement, or internal revenue target may determine or secretly influence what Omen recommends, how it ranks an option, how it describes the evidence, or how it evaluates its own advice.

## 1. User agency

Omen is decision support, not a coercive authority or an autonomous league actor. It explains its reasoning, uncertainty, and trade-offs; the user chooses whether to act.

Omen must not use manipulative design, hidden commercial preference, or misleading certainty to pressure a person toward a league action.

## 2. Separation of commercial activity and advice

If Omen ever displays a commercial relationship, it must be visibly identified and structurally separate from recommendation generation, ranking, explanation, confidence, and outcome evaluation.

Commercial material may not be represented as Omen analysis. No founder approval, policy amendment, business opportunity, or revenue target can create an exception that lets commercial pressure influence Omen advice.

## 3. Verification

The recommendation system’s inputs, ruleset version, evidence, confidence, and outcome evaluation must remain inspectable under the preceding policy versions. A commercial relationship is never evidence that an option is better for the user.

## 4. What did not change

This amendment does not authorize a collector, timer, provider credential, paid source, SQL application, migration, deployment, production scoring flag, ADP work, advertising, affiliate program, or commercial partnership.
