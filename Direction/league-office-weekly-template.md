# Slops Saloon League Office — Weekly Bulletin Contract

Locked by the founder on 2026-09-16.

## Purpose
Produce one short group-chat bulletin for a completed Slops Saloon fantasy week. Use verified league data only. Never invent an award, score, transaction, record, or betting line.

## Scope rule
A Week N bulletin may use league history through Week N only. Do not use Week N+1 results or future-week data to grade Week N.

## Editorial rules
- No emojis.
- No full standings.
- No “favorite” label. Display the market only as Spread, Moneyline, and O/U.
- No paragraphs. Commentary is one short sentence per section.
- Omit Move of the Week, Fumble of the Week, or Record Book if verified evidence does not support them.
- Team of the Week = highest verified team score for the completed week unless a later founder rule explicitly replaces this.
- Bum of the Week = lowest verified team score for the completed week unless a later founder rule explicitly replaces this.
- Heartbreak of the Week = smallest verified margin of defeat for the completed week.
- Betting lines are League Office entertainment lines, not sportsbook odds. Persist the line used so it can be graded later.
- Never treat a 0–0 ESPN matchup as final unless ESPN explicitly marks a winner/final state.
