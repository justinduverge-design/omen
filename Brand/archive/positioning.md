# Corvus Positioning

## One-Line Position

Corvus helps fantasy football players see their best move with clear, platform-aware reasoning.

## Audience

Fantasy football users who want confident weekly decisions without needing to become data scientists.

## Product Ladder

1. Trade Analyzer builds trust as the front door.
2. Draft Assistant helps users prepare for the season.
3. Omen of the Week / MVP Move becomes the main weekly decision moment.

## Differentiation

Corvus should combine fantasy football context, platform data, and plain-English explanation.

The user should leave with a decision, not a spreadsheet assignment.

## Platform Reality

Yahoo, Sleeper, and ESPN all matter.

ESPN is essential to the product promise, but it carries higher reliability and recovery risk than the other platforms.
