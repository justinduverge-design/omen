# Next Explainer Brief

Purpose: replace the current abstract teaser drafts with a clear app explainer that tells a first-time viewer what Omen does.

## Verdict On Current Drafts

- Music: not good enough. The generated beds are placeholders and should not be treated as final campaign audio.
- Message: too abstract. The videos show brand mood, not app value.
- Product clarity: weak. A viewer does not learn the workflow or why Omen matters.

## Better Video

Format: 25-35 seconds vertical first, with horizontal derived after.

Structure:

| Beat | Seconds | On screen | Copy |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Hook | 0-3 | Fantasy manager staring at trade/start decision | `Your league is guessing.` |
| Problem | 3-7 | Split cards: trade offer, start/sit, draft pick | `Every close call has hidden risk.` |
| Product | 7-13 | Omen UI-style panels: connect platform, read roster, matchup context | `Omen reads your league context.` |
| Recommendation | 13-21 | Recommendation card with confidence + risk labels | `Recommendation first. Confidence and risk right behind it.` |
| Outcome | 21-28 | Clear move selected, weak option fades | `See the move before the league does.` |
| CTA | 28-35 | Omen logo + waitlist | `Coming soon. Join the waitlist.` |

## Audio Direction

Use real licensed/free music, not generated placeholder audio:

- Snare-forward, but restrained.
- Sports trailer energy without EDM drops.
- 90-115 BPM.
- Clear downbeat for CTA.

Candidate search terms:

- `cinematic sports drums`
- `marching snare trailer`
- `dark sports hype`
- `percussion tension promo`

Record final track details in `assets/sound-credits.md`.
