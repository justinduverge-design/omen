# Corvus Roadmap

## MVP Goal

Create a polished Corvus app and website that clearly shows the value of the product and can be used as the foundation for launch.

## Product Priority Order

1. Verify the Trade Analyzer is built and working
2. Put Trade Analyzer front and center on the Corvus homepage
3. Create a strong example Trade Analyzer result on the homepage
4. Move Omen preview smaller and to the side
5. Move Draft Assistant preview smaller and to the side
6. Make Omen the best available weekly logic layer
7. Keep Start/Sit and Waiver Wire encapsulated by Omen
8. Keep ESPN, Yahoo, and Sleeper as required platform targets
9. Build an ESPN recovery playbook
10. Prepare launch/marketing once the app feels polished locally

## Feature 1: Trade Analyzer

Purpose:
Bring users into Corvus with a free, useful fantasy football tool.

Product role:
Front door.

Needs:
- Clear player input
- Trade comparison
- Example homepage result
- Plain-English recommendation
- Corvus Edge label
- Risk label
- CTA toward using Corvus / signing in

## Feature 2: Draft Assistant

Purpose:
Help users prepare during fantasy draft season.

Product role:
Couch / food / entertainment.

MVP shell can include:
- Draft strategy page
- Pick recommendation placeholder
- Player tiers placeholder
- ADP comparison
- Corvus rank

## Feature 3: Omen of the Week

Purpose:
Provide the best available weekly move Corvus can find.

Product role:
Main event.

Omen may include:
- Start/sit move
- Waiver pickup
- Trade angle
- Roster weakness
- Platform-specific opportunity
- Weekly matchup edge

## Encapsulated Features

Start/Sit and Waiver Wire are supporting logic paths inside Omen.

They can exist as tools in the app, but they should not compete with Trade Analyzer, Draft Assistant, or Omen on the homepage.

## Not Now

These are not current priorities:

- Hostinger app cutover
- Paid subscription system
- Full LLC/business setup
- Full social media automation
- Contractor handoff
- Native mobile app
- SportsRadar paid integration
