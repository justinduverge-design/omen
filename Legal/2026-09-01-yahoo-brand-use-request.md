# Yahoo brand-use request — draft for submission

**Status:** Ready to submit. Founder action.
**Submit at:** https://legal.yahoo.com/us/en/yahoo/permissions/branduseguidelines/index.html — the
Brand Use Guidelines page carries the request form. Yahoo states review takes **10 business days**
and that they are **under no obligation to respond**.
**Why we are asking:** Yahoo's guidelines require all specific uses of Yahoo Brand Features to be
approved in advance by Yahoo Brand Marketing. Our executed Fantasy API agreement obliges us to
*text* attribution; it does **not** grant logo rights. Those are separate permissions.

**Before sending, fill the bracketed fields.** Do not guess them — they are the parts that make the
request credible.

## Notes for the founder, not for Yahoo

- **Do not overstate the relationship.** The agreement is read-only data access. It is not a
  partnership, and describing it as one would be the exact thing their guidelines police.
- **The attribution sentence is contractual wording.** `known_issues.md` records that it is pinned
  by tests on both platforms and that "improving" it is a breach. Quote it exactly as above.
- **If they say no,** nothing breaks: the Yahoo tile stays the brand-colored letter mark it is
  today, which raises no permission question at all.
- **If they do not respond within 10 business days,** silence is not consent. Treat a
  non-response as a no.
