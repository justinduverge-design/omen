# Omen 0.1.0 (6) — what's new, and what to poke at

Thanks for testing. You don't need to know fantasy football to be useful here — most of
what changed this build is "does this look right," and you're better placed to answer that
than we are, because you're seeing it fresh.

If something looks wrong, it probably is. Send a screenshot.

---

## Please look at these first — they all changed today

**1. Light mode.** Put your phone in Light and open Command Center.
It used to be white cards on a white background with white lines between them. It should now
read warm, like paper, with cards that lift off the page. Does it?

**2. Dark mode.** Not black any more — smoky grey.
Better or worse than you remember? Genuinely asking, there's no right answer.

**3. The ESPN / Sleeper / Yahoo filter buttons** at the top of Command Center.
**Can you read all three?** Yahoo used to be dark purple text on a dark background and was
effectively invisible. They're now solid coloured pills with white text.

**4. Any box you type into** — sign-in, the emailed code boxes.
The outlines were nearly invisible. Can you tell where the box is *before* you tap it?

**5. Try both themes on the same screen.** Switch back and forth. Anything that looks fine in
one and broken in the other is exactly what we want to hear about.

---

## What actually works in this build

- **Signing in with a league provider, and connecting more than one league.** The league
  switcher is real — if you've got multiple leagues, you can move between them.
- **Your real team and league names**, not placeholders.
- **Waiver Watch — real.** Actual waiver data, not a mock-up.
- **Ledger — real.** Your actual move history.
- **Omens — real.** You should be getting genuine recommendations, not canned examples.

## What's half-built — don't waste time here

- **Trade.** It's rough and we know it. Feedback welcome but don't dig.
- **League page.** Barely touched yet. It looks unfinished because it is.

## Known gaps, so you don't report what we already know

- **Sleeper is the least-tested provider.** ESPN and Yahoo have been verified against real
  leagues; Sleeper hasn't yet. If you're on Sleeper and something's off, that's *very* useful
  to hear — it's a known soft spot, not a surprise.
- **Only a handful of screens have been checked visually**, and only on one physical phone
  (Justin's). Different phone, different screen size, different OS version — all fair game for
  weird findings.
- **Large text hasn't been re-checked this build.** If you run bigger text in your phone's
  accessibility settings and something gets cut off or overlaps, that's a real bug worth
  reporting, not you holding it wrong.

---

## What's most useful to send back

1. A screenshot, with **light or dark** mentioned.
2. What phone you're on.
3. What you expected vs. what you got — even "this just feels off" is worth sending. You
   noticing something is data even when you can't name it.
